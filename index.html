How I work

Most of my projects start the same way: break something complicated into simple pieces, then rebuild it carefully. In my low-poly cars that means polygons. In the type portrait it means words. In Desmos it means equations.

I’m interested in computer science and AI because I like that process of structure, iteration, and making something that works.
